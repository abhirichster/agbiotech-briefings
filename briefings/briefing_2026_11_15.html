Lead story

Perennial grasses show how crops could stop leaving nitrogen in the field

A Plant Cell study built a transcriptomic atlas of senescence from 2,685 RNA-seq libraries covering 14 field-grown Panicoideae grasses across two seasons, with maize and sorghum as annual references. The gene networks that strip nitrogen from ageing leaves turned out to be shared by annuals and perennials, but the networks that let perennials park that nitrogen in rhizomes and roots, linked to seed-like dormancy and desiccation tolerance, have diverged in annual crops. Because 30 to 50% of maize nitrogen stays in residue where it can be lost, the authors offer these conserved candidates as a route to perennial-style nutrient recycling in annual crops, though no engineered crop has yet been tested.

    The Plant Cell15 Nov 2026

    A 2,685-library atlas shows how perennial grasses bank nitrogen underground at senescence

    Researchers sequenced 2,685 RNA-seq libraries from field-grown leaves, roots, stalks and rhizomes of 14 Panicoideae grasses across two seasons, using maize and sorghum as annual references. Networks that move nitrogen out of senescing leaves were conserved between annuals and perennials, but networks that build underground sinks, tied to seed-like dormancy and desiccation tolerance, had diverged in annual crop accessions. The authors propose these genes as targets to cut the 30 to 50% of maize nitrogen left behind in residue.

    The Plant Cell15 Nov 2026

    A position-resolved regulatory map of plum ripening puts PsEIN3-1 at the top of a sugar cascade

    Combining transcriptomes, DNase-seq chromatin accessibility and DAP-seq for ripening transcription factors from six families, the study built a regulatory network for Prunus salicina fruit ripening. The ethylene factor PsEIN3-1 binds the promoters of PsbZIP44 and PsNAC1, which in turn target genes for ethylene synthesis, starch breakdown and sugar accumulation; the PsbZIP44 role was only preliminarily tested by transient transformation. Comparisons with peach, apricot and sweet cherry found conserved circuits, and the data are released as a Prunus Regulome Atlas.

    A CsCOP1, CsHY5 and CsGGT2 cascade sets theanine levels in tea

    QTL mapping in an albino tea population identified CsCOP1, and silencing it reduced theanine while overexpression raised it. CsCOP1 ubiquitinates CsHY5 at lysine 428, lowering HY5 protein, repressing the theanine-hydrolysing gene CsGGT2 and slowing theanine breakdown in leaves. A non-synonymous CsGGT2 variant changed hydrolysing activity in natural populations, giving a candidate marker for breeding high-theanine cultivars.

    HvNCED12, switched on by HvMYB10, supports salt tolerance in barley

    CRISPR knockout and overexpression lines of HvNCED12 in barley cv. Golden Promise were grown at 150 mM NaCl for 14 days. Salt induced the gene about sixfold within 24 hours; knockouts had less ABA and chlorophyll, 49.3% more malondialdehyde, poorer Na+/K+ balance and lower biomass, while overexpressors showed the opposite, and 100 µM ABA partly rescued the knockouts. HvMYB10 binds and activates the HvNCED12 promoter. These are controlled-environment results without field data.

    Spatial transcriptomics of the wheat grain points to crease FLA proteins in bran adhesion

    A spatial transcriptomic atlas of Triticum aestivum grain at 14 days post-anthesis split the seed into pericarp, starchy endosperm, transfer cells, crease and embryo, and found 4,682 region-exclusive gene loci across 1,751 functional categories. Fasciclin-like arabinogalactan protein (FLA) transcripts were concentrated in the crease and linked to cell wall remodelling genes. The authors suggest silencing crease FLAs could loosen bran from endosperm and raise flour extraction, a hypothesis not yet tested in milling trials.

    Sugar transporter HaSWEET14 partners with florigen HaFT4 to time sunflower flowering under low light

    Transcriptomes of sunflower grown at different light intensities identified HaSWEET14, a SWEET sugar transporter strongly suppressed by low light. In transient assays, silencing HaSWEET14 delayed flowering by 8 days and silencing HaFT4 by about 10 days, while overexpression of either sped flowering. The two proteins interact at the plasma membrane, suggesting a sugar transporter to florigen link in shade-delayed flowering of this oil crop.

    Mycorrhizal fungi did not shield native grasses from combined heat and drought

    Six Australian native grasses with C3 or C4 photosynthesis were exposed to a simulated heatwave with and without water stress, with and without arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi. Combined heat and drought did more damage than either stress alone, and AM fungi did not improve performance, instead lowering transpiration under stress. C4 grasses outperformed C3 grasses, and both were more resilient in spring than in winter.

    NtWRKY11 raises cold tolerance in tobacco through ABA, calcium and CBF signalling

    This commentary highlights a study by Bedwell and colleagues showing a mutualism meltdown in legume and rhizobium symbioses after 33 years of nitrogen fertilisation. Long-read sequencing revealed a mixed community of nodulating and non-nodulating Rhizobiaceae, in which a beneficial symbiont fell both in abundance and in the share of host-beneficial alleles.     The Plant Genome14 Nov 2026

    RAGNAROK, a GPU-accelerated Nextflow pipeline, beats BRAKER3 on plant gene annotation

    RAGNAROK bundles ab initio prediction, transcript and protein evidence, repeat masking, functional assignment and quality checks into one reproducible Nextflow and Apptainer workflow. Across seven reference-quality plant genomes it achieved higher sensitivity, precision and F1 than BRAKER3 at exon, transcript and gene levels when UTRs were included, ran faster, and improved BUSCO completeness when re-annotating five Rosaceae genomes. The code is open on GitHub; the benchmark was run by the tool's developers.

The team built a cut-dipping hairy root transformation system with up to 82.22% efficiency and used it to show that silencing miR319 promoted root elongation under high but not low nitrogen.     SLAF-seq of 45 Selenicereus accessions yielded 1,206,498 high-quality SNPs, resolved three genetic groups and corrected several misclassified accessions.     A new Alternaria forma specialis causes leaf spot on California lettuce

    A leaf spot with concentric rings appeared on coastal California lettuce in 2023, and all 15 tested iceberg, romaine, leaf and butterhead cultivars were susceptible. Of 22 other crops in seven families, only endive, potato and tomato developed disease, and genome sequencing placed the pathogen as a new forma specialis within the potato early blight species complex, distinct from the potato pathogen. The fungus forms microsclerotia that can start infection, and a near telomere-to-telomere genome is provided.
