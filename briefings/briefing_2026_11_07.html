Lead story

Field-grown GM bananas carry nutritionally relevant iron and zinc, but with a yield cost

A New Phytologist field evaluation in Australia grew 450 genetically modified Cavendish and Lady Finger banana plants carrying six iron-homeostasis transgenes. Fruit iron and zinc reached 45.5 and 51.9 mg per kg dry weight with OsNAS expression, and 28 lines supplied at least 50% of the estimated average requirement of iron for children. Higher mineral content correlated with lower yield, partly eased by co-expressing OsVIT1. Results are from the first planting crop only and are not yet East African field data.

    Strigolactone receptor D14 integrates phosphate and strigolactone signaling by stabilizing PHR1

    Under low phosphate, strigolactone-induced anthocyanin needs PHR1, and the strigolactone receptor D14 binds PHR1 in a strigolactone-dependent way. The interaction promotes PHR1 dimerization, blocks its ubiquitination-mediated degradation and boosts its activity, so the complex switches on strigolactone and anthocyanin biosynthesis genes. This explains why strigolactone has opposite effects on lateral roots under low and high phosphorus. The work is in Arabidopsis; peer-reviewed, abstract read.

    HvMAPK5 phosphorylates HvWRKY75 to switch on HvHKT1;2 and strengthen barley salt tolerance

    The kinase HvMAPK5 phosphorylates the transcription factor HvWRKY75 at Ser21, which stabilises it and raises its activation of the sodium transporter gene HvHKT1;2. Overexpression of HvHKT1;2 increased sodium efflux at the root apex, cut shoot sodium and raised the K/Na ratio, while knockdown did the opposite. Peer-reviewed lab-scale work in barley, with no field yield data in the abstract.

    Single-nucleus atlas of peanut stem rot points to AhWRKY40 as a resistance regulator

    Profiling 29,308 nuclei and 52,819 expressed genes, the study found that infection with Sclerotium rolfsii mostly downregulated genes and shrank every cell population, with epidermal cells carrying the highest share of resistance genes. Overexpressing AhWRKY40 raised resistance and increased jasmonic and salicylic acid. Peer-reviewed; transgenic validation was not described as field-scale.

    A potassium channel beta-subunit blocks potyvirus replication and movement by binding 6K2

    Nicotiana benthamiana NbKNB1 binds the turnip mosaic virus 6K2 protein, disrupting 6K2 self-association and vesicle maturation, and competes with the viral CI protein to limit cell-to-cell movement.     Nineteen years of salinity raise wheat crown rot; non-saline irrigation cut it by 49%

    Severe salinity raised soil pH from 7.64 to 8.38, cut available phosphorus by 54.11% and available potassium by 37.69%, and lifted the crown rot disease index from 10.51 to 32.88 while shrinking microbial diversity. Three years of non-saline irrigation lowered the index by 49.17%. Field data from China; published 23 October.
