Lead story

Stripe rust inoculum in Sichuan may come via a diverse Qamdo population in eastern Tibet

Population genomics and virulence tests on 220 Puccinia striiformis f. sp. tritici isolates from 2025 found 66 known and 76 additional races on the Chinese differentials, and 148 races on single-gene differentials. The Qamdo population in eastern Tibet had the highest diversity and strong gene flow with Sichuan populations, so oversummering southeastern Tibet populations may seed Sichuan epidemics. The data are from Chinese populations, and the link to Prairie stripe rust is not tested.

    SiTIFY9 raises salt tolerance and brings forward flowering in foxtail millet

    Overexpressing SiTIFY9 cut hydrogen peroxide and malondialdehyde, raised catalase and peroxidase activity and proline, and lowered Na accumulation while keeping more K, giving a higher K/Na ratio under salt. Transcriptome data point to changes in redox, ion transport and phenylpropanoid genes, and the lines also flowered earlier. Peer-reviewed, abstract read; lab and controlled conditions, no field data.
