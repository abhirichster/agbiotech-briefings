Lead story

First SDHI fungicide resistance found in the canola blackleg fungus Leptosphaeria maculans

A decade-long Australian survey has detected the first SDHI-resistant Leptosphaeria maculans reported anywhere. Three single-nucleotide changes, SdhB H277Y, SdhB H277L and SdhD D144G, were each confirmed by genetic mapping, sequencing and functional complementation to reduce SDHI sensitivity. The authors note reliance on fungicides for blackleg has grown and developed molecular markers for all three mutations to monitor resistance. The data are from Australian populations, and field consequences for Prairie canola are not tested.
