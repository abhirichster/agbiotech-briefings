Lead story

Three in four Chinese sprangletop populations now shrug off a key rice grass herbicide, mostly by breaking it down

A survey of 101 Leptochloa chinensis populations from rice fields across four Yangtze provinces found 75.3% resistant to the ACCase inhibitor cyhalofop-butyl, a third of them highly so. Known target-site mutations in ACCase explained only 16 of the 47 moderately or highly resistant populations, while cytochrome P450 and glutathione S-transferase metabolism was implicated in 27 of the 31 resistant populations lacking such mutations (87.1%). Two years of field trials then pointed to the HPPD inhibitor pyraquinate as the best replacement, with three other HPPD inhibitors as rotation partners. Metabolic resistance matters beyond China because it can cross herbicide groups that farmers rotate between, the same concern that hangs over Group 1 resistant wild oat on the Prairies.

    Maize enzyme ZmGPAT6 throttles hydrogen peroxide transport and leaves plants exposed to salt-alkali stress

    ZmGPAT6 encodes a glycerol-3-phosphate acyltransferase with both acyltransferase and phosphatase activity; it lowers phosphatidic acid, which normally binds and activates the kinases ZmMPK2 and ZmMPK7 that phosphorylate the aquaporin ZmPIP2;1 so it can move hydrogen peroxide. Salt and alkali stress both induce ZmGPAT6, which also directly dephosphorylates the kinases and the aquaporin, so hydrogen peroxide over-accumulates in leaves and roots and growth suffers. The authors put forward the module as a genetic target for salt-alkali tolerant maize.

    Field-grown cotton does form aerenchyma under waterlogging, including a previously undescribed secondary type

    Cotton has long been thought unable to make aerenchyma, but root sections from waterlogged field plants (cv. RCH773BGII) showed it clearly, plus secondary aerenchyma arising from secondary phloem parenchyma, cambium and periderm. Waterlogging raised hydrogen peroxide by 28.9% and cut membrane thermal stability by 16.9% and soluble protein by 26.0%; the ethylene precursor ACC and the perception blocker 1-MCP partly restored membrane stability (+8.4% and +10.5%). 1-MCP also raised starch by 48.3% and guaiacol peroxidase by 47.6%, which the authors propose as a field strategy for waterlogging tolerance.

    SUPERMAN helps build seeds in Arabidopsis, Medicago and tomato alike

    Comparing Arabidopsis thaliana, Medicago truncatula and Solanum lycopersicum, the C2H2 transcription factor SUPERMAN and its orthologs consistently mark integuments and the micropylar and chalazal endosperm, though timing and tissue context differ by species. Mutants show altered embryogenesis and more seed abortion in Arabidopsis, impaired early seed development in Medicago, and ovule defects in tomato. In Arabidopsis the defects track altered auxin responsiveness, pointing to a role in hormone-mediated signalling between maternal and filial seed tissues.

    In poplar, PagMYBS1 curbs wood formation by degrading cytokinin and weakens drought tolerance through PagERF113

    The endodermis-expressed R2R3-MYB factor PagMYBS1 in 84K poplar, when overexpressed, reduced plant height, xylem width, fibre length and secondary wall thickness while raising vessel density. It binds GATAA boxes to activate the cytokinin oxidase gene PagCKX1 and also induces PagERF113; PagERF113 overexpression reduced drought tolerance whereas RNAi lines were more resilient. Downstream, PagERF113 activates PagMPK3, PagWRKY33 and PagPR-3, linking MAPK signalling and amino sugar metabolism to cell wall remodelling.

    A wheat grain weight gene and a regulatory SNP 24.4 kb upstream hand breeders a diagnostic marker

    Phenotyping 120 modern bread wheat cultivars over two seasons and resequencing them against the gap-free Chinese Spring telomere-to-telomere reference gave 6,313,343 high-confidence SNPs, and GWAS combined with selective sweep scans mapped TaPTC52 to a 646.3 to 646.8 Mb interval on chromosome 5A. A regulatory SNP 24.4 kb upstream of the transcription start site separates the favourable Hap1 (T allele) from Hap2 (C allele); Hap1 accessions had significantly higher thousand-grain weight and higher TaPTC52 transcript levels (both P less than 0.0001). Pedigree tracking showed Hap1 reaching close to 90% frequency in modern elite lines descended from Zhoumai 16 and Zhoumai 22.

    Co-expressing the tapetum degeneration regulator TDR with the red fluorescent protein gene DsRed2 gave DMS-TD rice plants that were completely and stably male sterile across genetic backgrounds and environments, with the trait inherited by progeny and fluorescence used to sort sterile from fertile seed before sowing.     Peach palm genomes grew more uniform, not larger, as the crop spread from its domestication centre

    Flow cytometry and RepeatExplorer2 analysis of 15 accessions, covering the wild progenitor Bactris gasipaes var. chichagui type 1 and four domesticated landraces of var. gasipaes, found no difference in genome size among the taxa, but genome size variability fell as distance from the southwestern Amazonian centre of domestication increased (R squared of 0.975, p less than 0.001). Transposable elements made up about 70% of the genome in all taxa, and the Angela and Ale (Ty1/Copia) families drove most of the repeat variation and were less abundant in domesticated landraces. The authors call for validation with broader sampling and genome-wide data.
