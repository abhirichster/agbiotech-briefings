Lead story

Fusarium pan-effectome maps 4,057 effector families behind wheat head blight

A PNAS paper released 3 November assembles 4,057 effector families from 188 genomes of 27 Fusarium species, with 235 families conserved in at least 95% of isolates. Of 44 families showing adaptive evolution, most altered wheat immune responses, and CRISPR-Cas9 deletions showed several conserved effectors drive cultivar-dependent head blight severity. This is a basic-science resource, not a resistant variety, but it points breeders to conserved targets. Abstract only was read.

    PNAS3 Nov 2026

    A pan-effectome of Fusarium head blight fungi shows conserved effectors shape host specificity

    Researchers built a catalogue of 4,057 effector families from 188 genomes across 27 species of the Fusarium complex, including a soft-core set of 235 families found in at least 95% of isolates. Of these, 44 families show signs of adaptive evolution, and most candidates tested changed wheat immune responses. CRISPR-Cas9 deletions showed that several conserved effectors affect head blight severity in a cultivar-dependent way. Peer-reviewed; abstract only read.

    Tartary buckwheat FtMYB15 coordinates cadmium uptake and detoxification

    The transcription factor FtMYB15 binds the promoters of the zinc transporter-like gene FtZIP3 and the glutathione transferase gene FtGSTU19, raising cadmium influx and detoxification together. Evidence comes from buckwheat hairy roots, and FtGSTU19 also raised cadmium tolerance in Arabidopsis, so whole-plant field behaviour is untested. Peer-reviewed; abstract only read.
