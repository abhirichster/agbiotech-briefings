Lead story

Optimized prime editing recreates and stacks disease resistance alleles in tomato

A Plant Cell paper published on 5 November describes three tomato prime editors, TomPE2, TomPE6c and TomPE7, that precisely recreated a pepper resistance deletion in SlBs5 and SlBs5L and raised resistance to Xanthomonas. Stop codons in SlDMR6 gave resistance to both Xanthomonas and Pseudomonas syringae, and a modular system can edit several loci at once. TomPE6c gave the highest share of precise edits. It is peer-reviewed lab and greenhouse work; no field data or yield figures are reported in the abstract.
