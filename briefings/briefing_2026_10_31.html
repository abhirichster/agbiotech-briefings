Lead story

AI-designed gene editor used to make rice resistant to bacterial blight

University of Missouri researchers adapted OpenCRISPR-1, an AI-designed gene-editing protein, to make rice resistant to bacterial blight, according to a 28 October Crop Biotech Update summary of a paper in aBIOTECH. The plants still need further testing and regulatory review before any commercial use. Only a search summary was read, so no resistance figures are given here.

    The SCHENGEN pathway links pathogen recognition to lignin barriers via BIK1

    Authors report that the peptides CIF1 and CIF2 and their receptor SGN3 are induced by avirulent bacterial infection and are needed for pathogen-induced lignification and disease resistance, with SGN3 phosphorylating BIK1.
