    Salk ROOTS project tests deeper rooted soybeans in the field

    The three year, $18 million ROOTS project, funded by the Bezos Earth Fund, takes lab engineered soybeans to field sites in Illinois, Missouri, Kansas and Iowa. It builds on 347 candidate genes for deeper roots, and Salk estimates one hectare could store about one extra metric ton of CO2 per year. Those carbon figures are projections from the researchers, and first field results are only expected this fall. Coverage dates from 18 September to early October.
